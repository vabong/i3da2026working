Apple Joins I3DA 2026 as Platinum Sponsor

13 September 2026

I3DA is delighted to announce Apple as Platinum Sponsor of the International Conference on Immersive and 3D Audio 2026. We are grateful for their support, which will help us bring this special edition of the conference to Venice in November.

I3DA 2026 takes place from 19 to 22 November at Palazzo Giustinian Lolin. This edition widens the conference beyond spatial audio to the visual and interactive dimensions of immersive media, from virtual acoustics and auralization to digital archaeology, photogrammetry and immersive world building. Support at this level allows us to keep that programme open to researchers and practitioners from across the field.

Apple joins MH Acoustics LLC among our confirmed sponsors. Details of every sponsor, and of how to partner with the conference, are on the sponsors page. Registration for I3DA 2026 is open now.
