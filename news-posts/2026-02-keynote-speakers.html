First Keynote Speakers Announced for I3DA 2026

February 2026

We are delighted to announce the first three keynote speakers for I3DA 2026. This initial selection reflects the conference’s commitment to interdisciplinary excellence across immersive audio, sonic heritage, soundscape research, and spatial practices.

Angela Bellia, Full Professor at the University of Palermo, is internationally recognised for her work in musicology, sonic heritage, sound studies, and digital approaches to music research. Her scholarship bridges historical inquiry with contemporary methodologies, offering critical perspectives on how sound, space, and culture intersect across time.

David Monacchi is a composer, researcher, and pioneer in the field of eco-acoustics. His work focuses on the sonic complexity of primary forests and fragile ecosystems worldwide, combining scientific research, advanced recording technologies, and immersive artistic practices to address urgent questions of biodiversity, ecology, and listening.

Tin Oberman is a researcher, urban planner, architect, and musician whose work explores the relationship between sound, space, and the built environment. He obtained his M.Arch. and PhD in Architecture and Urbanism at the University of Zagreb, where he also served as Research and Teaching Assistant. His academic trajectory includes post-doctoral research in urban and spatial planning, a visiting appointment at the Acoustics Group of the School of Architecture at the University of Sheffield, and, since 2018, a research position at the Bartlett Institute for Environmental Design and Engineering at University College London. There, he contributes to the ERC-funded project “Soundscape Indices,” integrating architectural research with soundscape theory and practice.

Additional keynote speakers will be announced soon.
