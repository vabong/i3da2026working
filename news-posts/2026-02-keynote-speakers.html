First Keynote Speakers Announced for I3DA 2026

February 2026

Angela Bellia is Full Professor of Musicology and Music History at the University of Palermo. Her research focuses on sonic heritage, soundscape archaeology, sound studies, heritage acoustics, and digital approaches to musicological research. She has conducted research at leading international institutions and has led major funded projects exploring the role of sound in cultural heritage contexts.

David Monacchi is a composer, researcher, and pioneer in the field of eco-acoustics. His work investigates the sonic complexity of primary forests and fragile ecosystems worldwide, combining scientific research, advanced recording techniques, and immersive artistic practices. His research addresses critical questions of biodiversity, ecology, and the role of listening in environmental awareness.

Tin Oberman is a researcher, urban planner, architect, and musician. He holds an M.Arch. and PhD in Architecture and Urbanism from the University of Zagreb and has held research and teaching positions in Croatia and the UK. Currently based at University College London, his work explores soundscape research, spatial planning, and the relationship between sound, architecture, and urban environments.
