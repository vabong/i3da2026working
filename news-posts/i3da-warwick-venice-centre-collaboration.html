Collaboration with University of Warwick Venice Centre

27 January 2026

We are pleased to announce a collaboration with the University of Warwick Venice Centre. In this special edition of the International Conference in Immersive and 3D Audio, the conference will take place in Venice in the historic headquarters of the Fondazione Ugo e Olga Levi and in the Warwick Venice Centre on the second floor of the Palazzo Giustinian Lolin.

The University of Warwick is the only British University to have a fixed presence in Venice, and has been teaching students in Italy for nearly 60 years. The Warwick Venice Centre (WVC) offers students at Warwick a unique immersive opportunity to study in Venice, focussing on subjects including History, History of Art, Business Studies, Liberal Arts, Sustainability, Classics, and more. Since its inception, Warwick has always been an outward-looking and global university; they continue to build strong partnerships both internationally and within Europe to boost learning, research, and provide opportunities for our students and staff.

The WVC is a key venue for sharing some of the University’s world-leading research via conferences and workshops and fostering innovative collaborations with Warwick’s global partners, demonstrating the University’s commitment to meaningful engagement on the international stage. The Marco Polo International Programme, hosted at the centre in 2024, was shortlisted by Times Higher Education for the International Collaboration of the Year.

The Warwick Venice Centre is eager to support and participate in the I3DA conference. It looks forward to welcoming all delegates to Venice.
