Travel & Funding Support

Participants in I3DA 2026 may be eligible to apply for external mobility funding to support travel and accommodation costs. The conference itself does not administer travel grants; however, we are pleased to highlight the following European funding opportunity.

Culture Moves Europe (Creative Europe)

Culture Moves Europe is a mobility funding programme of the European Commission (Creative Europe) supporting artists and cultural professionals working across music, sound, performance, and cultural heritage. Participation in I3DA 2026 — including presenting a paper, artwork, performance, demonstration, or engaging in professional exchange — may form the basis of an eligible mobility project.

  • International travel allowance
  • Daily subsistence support during the stay
  • Additional top-ups (e.g. green travel, accessibility needs)

Applications are made directly by participants to the Culture Moves Europe programme. The I3DA 2026 Organising Committee can provide a letter of invitation and confirmation of participation upon request.

Eligibility is determined by the funding body and typically applies to artists and cultural professionals legally residing in Creative Europe countries.
More information: culture.ec.europa.eu/culture-moves-europe
