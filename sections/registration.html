Registration Travel & Funding Support

Registration – Onsite Attendance

Onsite registration includes full in-person access to the conference programme in Venice, as well as social and catering activities throughout the event.

  • Full attendance to all scientific sessions
  • Certificate of attendance
  • Submission of one paper
  • Coffee breaks and lunches
  • Social dinner

Registration – Remote Attendance

Remote registration is intended for participants who wish to attend the conference online and present their work remotely.

* Student registration: applies exclusively to Ph.D. candidates, research fellows, and registered students.

** Extra Paper: fee applies to each additional paper submission. One paper is included in the registration fee.

VAT: All fees are subject to 22% VAT. VAT exemption applies only to Italian participants whose registration is paid directly by a public institution.

Travel & Funding Support

Participants in I3DA 2026 may be eligible to apply for external mobility funding to support travel and accommodation costs. While the conference itself does not administer travel grants, we outline below several established funding routes that may support attendance.

European Union

Culture Moves Europe (Creative Europe) is a mobility funding programme of the European Commission supporting artists and cultural professionals legally residing in Creative Europe countries and active in eligible sectors such as Music and Cultural Heritage.

  • International travel allowance
  • Daily subsistence support
  • Top-ups (green travel, accessibility, visa-related costs)

Participation in I3DA 2026 (e.g. presentation, demonstration, workshop, artistic or professional exchange) may form the basis of an eligible mobility project. Applications are made directly by participants. The Organising Committee can provide letters of invitation on request.

culture.ec.europa.eu/culture-moves-europe

Additional Travel Support (UK & US Participants)

For UK-based participants
  • Royal Academy of Engineering – International Travel Grants Individual support for international conference and workshop travel.
  • British Academy – Conference Grants Small grants supporting attendance at international conferences in the arts and humanities.
  • Travel bursaries offered by professional societies (e.g. AES UK, Institute of Acoustics) may also be available.
For US-based participants
  • Many US universities offer departmental or graduate-school conference travel grants to support faculty, postdoctoral researchers, and PhD students.
  • Audio Engineering Society (AES) – Travel Awards Conference travel and registration support for AES members.
  • College Art Association (CAA) – Conference Travel Grants Travel and registration support for artists and artist-scholars.
