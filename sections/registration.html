Travel & Funding Support

Participation Access Support

To support equitable participation, a limited number of registration fee adjustments are available for participants travelling long distances or registering without institutional funding.

Adjustments are determined on a case-by-case basis and applied directly to the registration fee. This measure does not constitute a travel grant, reimbursement, or cash payment. Availability is limited.

European Union Mobility Funding

I3DA 2026 does not administer travel grants directly. However, participants may be eligible to apply independently for external mobility funding to support travel and accommodation. Applications are submitted directly by participants to the Culture Moves Europe programme. The I3DA 2026 Organising Committee can provide a letter of invitation or confirmation of participation upon request.

Culture Moves Europe (Creative Europe Programme)

Culture Moves Europe is a mobility funding programme of the European Commission. It supports artists and cultural professionals legally residing in a Creative Europe country and active in eligible sectors such as Music and Cultural Heritage.

Participation in I3DA 2026 — including presenting a paper, artwork, demonstration, workshop, or engaging in professional exchange — may form the basis of an eligible mobility project, provided the application is framed within an artistic or cultural context.

  • International travel allowance
  • Daily subsistence support
  • Additional top-ups (green travel, accessibility, visa-related costs)

Applications framed solely as attendance at a technical or academic conference, without a clear artistic or cultural dimension, may be considered ineligible.

More information: culture.ec.europa.eu/culture-moves-europe
