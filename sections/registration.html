Travel & Funding Support

Participants in I3DA 2026 may be eligible to apply for external mobility funding to support travel and accommodation costs. While the conference itself does not administer travel grants, we are pleased to highlight a European funding opportunity relevant to artists and cultural professionals working in immersive audio and related fields.

Additional Travel Support (UK & US Participants)

For UK-based participants
  • Royal Academy of Engineering – International Travel Grants
    Supports international travel and attendance at conferences and workshops for UK-based engineers.
  • British Academy – Conference Grants
    Small grants supporting travel and participation in international conferences in the arts and humanities.
  • Travel bursaries offered by professional societies (e.g. AES UK, Institute of Acoustics) may also be available.
For US-based participants
  • Many US universities offer departmental or graduate-school conference travel grants to support faculty, postdoctoral researchers, and PhD students.
  • Audio Engineering Society (AES) – Travel Awards
    Conference travel and registration support for AES members, particularly students and early-career professionals.
  • College Art Association (CAA) – Conference Travel Grants
    Travel and registration support for artists and artist-scholars attending international conferences.
