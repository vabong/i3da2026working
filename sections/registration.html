Travel & Funding Support

Participants in I3DA 2026 may be eligible to apply for external mobility funding to support travel and accommodation costs. While the conference itself does not administer travel grants, we are pleased to highlight a European funding opportunity relevant to artists and cultural professionals working in immersive audio and related fields.

Culture Moves Europe (Creative Europe)

Culture Moves Europe is a mobility funding programme of the European Commission (Creative Europe). The call is open to artists and cultural professionals legally residing in a Creative Europe country and active in eligible sectors such as Music and Cultural Heritage. Participation in I3DA 2026 — including presenting a paper, artwork, performance, demonstration, workshop, or engaging in professional exchange — may form the basis of an eligible mobility project.

  • International travel allowance
  • Daily subsistence support during the stay
  • Additional top-ups (e.g. green travel, accessibility needs)

Applications are made directly by participants to the Culture Moves Europe programme. The I3DA 2026 Organising Committee can provide a letter of invitation and confirmation of participation upon request.

Quick self-check (to avoid wasted applications)

  • Likely relevant if your work is situated in music/sound practice, performance, installations, immersive storytelling, or heritage listening and auralisation contexts.
  • Less likely if your application is framed purely as academic conference travel for technical dissemination.
  • Required: legal residence in a Creative Europe country and active involvement in an eligible cultural sector.

Guidance for engineers, researchers, and doctoral candidates

Applicants with primarily engineering or research profiles (e.g. DSP, acoustics, XR, signal processing, computer science) are advised to frame their mobility project in terms of practice-based, artistic, or cultural exchange — such as demonstrations, listening sessions, installations, or applied work in heritage and performance contexts — rather than purely technical dissemination.

  • Emphasise engagement with artistic practice and creative experimentation
  • Describe professional exchange with artists, composers, and interdisciplinary practitioners
  • Highlight applied or experiential aspects of your work
  • Position conference participation within a broader cultural or creative development process

Projects framed solely as attendance at an academic or technical conference, without a clear artistic or cultural dimension, may be considered ineligible.

If you are unsure about your eligibility, we strongly recommend consulting the Culture Moves Europe call documentation or contacting the Culture Moves Europe team directly before applying.

More information: culture.ec.europa.eu/culture-moves-europe
