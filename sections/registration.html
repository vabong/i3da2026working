Travel & Funding Support

I3DA 2026 does not administer travel grants directly. However, participants may be eligible to apply independently for external mobility funding to support travel and accommodation. Applications are submitted directly by participants to the Culture Moves Europe programme. The I3DA 2026 Organising Committee can provide a letter of invitation or confirmation of participation upon request. The funding route outlined below is provided for information only.

European Union — Culture Moves Europe

Culture Moves Europe is a mobility funding programme of the European Commission (Creative Europe). It supports artists and cultural professionals legally residing in a Creative Europe country and active in eligible sectors such as Music and Cultural Heritage.

Participation in I3DA 2026 — including presenting a paper, artwork, demonstration, workshop, or engaging in professional exchange — may form the basis of an eligible mobility project, provided the application is framed within an artistic or cultural context.

  • International travel allowance
  • Daily subsistence support
  • Additional top-ups (e.g. green travel, accessibility, visa-related costs)

Applications are submitted directly by participants to the Culture Moves Europe programme. The I3DA 2026 Organising Committee can provide a letter of invitation or confirmation of participation upon request.

Applications framed solely as attendance at a technical or academic conference, without a clear artistic or cultural dimension, may be considered ineligible.

More information: culture.ec.europa.eu/culture-moves-europe

Participation Access Support (Registration Fee Adjustment)

A limited number of registration fee adjustments are available to support participants travelling long distances or registering without institutional funding. Adjustments are determined on a case-by-case basis and applied directly to the registration fee.

Support does not constitute a travel grant, reimbursement, or cash payment. Availability is limited. Applicants will be notified individually of the outcome.
