Keynote Speakers

Angela Bellia
University of Palermo, Italy
Angela Bellia is Full Professor of Musicology and Music History in the Department of Cultures and Societies at the University of Palermo. She earned her MA and PhD at the University of Bologna and completed her classic piano and opera singer studies at the Italian Conservatories of Music. Her research interests span sonic heritage, soundscape archaeology, sound studies, heritage acoustics, computational archaeomusicology, and digital heritage.

Her research has been conducted at institutions including New York University, EHESS Paris, the University of Zürich, and the National Research Council of Italy. Supported by Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, she has led major European research initiatives.

She is Principal Investigator of the AURAL project and recipient of an Advanced Grant from the Italian Science Fund (FIS) for the VOICING project. Her contributions have been recognized with the MCAA “Outstanding Contributor Award” (2021).
David Monacchi
Ecoacoustics · Composer · Interdisciplinary Artist
David Monacchi is an ecoacoustics researcher, composer, and interdisciplinary artist. For over 25 years he has developed Fragments of Extinction, conducting field research in the world’s remaining undisturbed primary equatorial forests.

His work combines unique 3D soundscape recordings with science-based sound art to raise awareness of the biodiversity crisis. His Eco-acoustic Theatre, touring internationally as SONOSFERA®, has been presented in museums and public spaces worldwide.
