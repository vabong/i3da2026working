Keynote Speakers

Angela Bellia
University of Palermo, Italy
Angela Bellia is Full Professor of Musicology and Music History in the Department of Cultures and Societies at the University of Palermo. She earned her MA and PhD at the University of Bologna and completed her classic piano and opera singer studies at the Italian Conservatories of Music. Her research interests span sonic heritage, soundscape archaeology, sound studies, heritage acoustics, computational archaeomusicology, and digital heritage.

Her research has been conducted at prestigious institutions, including the Institute of Fine Arts (New York University), the École des Hautes Etudes en Sciences Sociales (Paris), the University of Zürich, and the National Research Council of Italy (CNR). Her research activities have been supported by the European Union program Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA).

She has been the Principal Investigator of the AURAL project and is the recipient of a recently awarded Advanced Grant from the Italian Science Fund (FIS) for the VOICING project, funded by the Ministry of University and Research (MUR). Angela Bellia has been invited or selected to present at over 150 peer-reviewed international conferences and advanced schools across multiple continents.

She serves as Editor in Chief of the TELESTES series and of Telestes: An International Journal of Archaeomusicology and Archaeology of Sound. As the Chair and founding member of the Archaeomusicology Interest Group (AMIG) of the AIA, she has organized numerous international workshops and colloquia for their annual meetings. She is the author of numerous books and contributions published by prestigious academic publishers, including Bloomsbury, Brill, Fabrizio Serra, Franz Steiner Verlag, Oxbow, and Routledge.

Her contributions to promoting European research have been recognized with the MCAA "Outstanding Contributor Award" (2021).
David Monacchi
Ecoacoustics • Composer • Interdisciplinary Artist
David Monacchi (Italy, 1970) is an ecoacoustics researcher, composer and interdisciplinary artist. He has been developing the project Fragments of Extinction for 25 years, conducting field research in the world's remaining areas of undisturbed primary equatorial forest in all continents. The recipient of multiple international awards for his work on intangible heritage of ecosystems, Monacchi is pioneering a cross-disciplinary approach based on unique 3D soundscape recordings, to raise awareness on the biodiversity crisis through science-based sound art.

His Eco-acoustic Theatre's patented invention (a spherical amphitheatre dedicated to sensorial and cognitive deep listening experiences) is being built in museums and public spaces, now traveling as SONOSFERA®. Monacchi has been portrayed by journals as Nature and PNAS, and is now full-professor at the State Conservatory of Pesaro, Italy. Collaborations with IUCN and ICCROM, to promote advocacy in primary forest soundscape conservation, culminated in an invited talk for the plenary session of the UN COP-14 Biodiversity Conference and for UN COP-27 Climate Change Conference.
