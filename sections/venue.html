Conference Venue

The I3DA 2026 international conference will take place in Venice, at Palazzo Giustinian Lolin, the headquarters of Fondazione Ugo e Olga Levi and University of Warwick Venice Centre.

Fondazione Ugo e Olga Levi ©

The archive of the Fondazione Ugo e Olga Levi preserves an important collection of historical music manuscripts, printed scores, books, and documents dedicated to the study and preservation of Venetian and Italian musical heritage. Alongside its scholarly activities, the Foundation promotes the dissemination of musical knowledge through a significant publishing programme devoted to academic publications, scholarly series, and musicological editions.

Getting Around

Venice is a compact, walkable city. The conference venue is easily reachable on foot or by vaporetto, and November visitors are not subject to the city's day-tripper access fee.

Walking

From Santa Lucia station or Piazzale Roma, the venue is a pleasant 25–30 minute walk through the city, crossing the Accademia bridge over the Grand Canal.

Vaporetto

Lines 1 and 2 run along the Grand Canal. Lines 2 and 4.1 connect Giudecca island to the main island. A single ticket costs €9.50; multi-day passes offer better value — 48hr €30, 72hr €40.

Access Fee

Venice operates a day-tripper access fee on selected dates. November is not affected, so conference attendees will not be required to pay any entry charge.
