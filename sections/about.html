I3DA: Immersive Media in Heritage, Nature and Consciousness

I3DA 2026 will again focus on innovation in 3D immersive audio, including virtual acoustics, architectural and musical acoustics, subjective evaluations and listening tests, immersive soundscape design, and creative uses of immersive technologies. Furthermore, I3DA 2026 will be a special edition, that will include visual and interactive aspects of immersive media, such as related computer graphics, LIDAR scans, photogrammetry, immersive world building, digital archaeology, 3D visualization, and hardware and software applications across both fields. The aim is to open new possibilities for collaboration, networking, and deeper understanding between complementary sensory domains in immersive media.

I3DA 2026 invites academic and industrial researchers to share their latest research, methods, and demonstrations related to immersive and 3D audio, audiovisual interaction, and spatial media technologies. Topics include virtual and augmented reality and their applications, digital archaeology, musical acoustics, acoustic signal processing, nonlinear acoustics in auralization, machine learning for immersive media, heritage acoustics, psychoacoustics, game audio, sound and media arts, as well as bioacoustics and environmental acoustics. Contributions beyond these areas that align with the conference theme are also welcome.

The International Conference on Immersive and 3D Audio – I3DA 2026 will take place in Venice at Palazzo Giustinian Lolin, in the San Marco district, a historic building that hosts internationally renowned academic and cultural institutions.

The conference I3DA 2026 is realized through collaboration between the University of Bologna, Department of Architecture with the Fondazione Ugo e Olga Levi and the University of Warwick Venice Centre.

I3DA will be indexed on IEEE like the the previous years, with Special Issues.

The conference will represent the forefront of new methodologies and technologies in the field, establishing itself as a high-profile international meeting point for researchers and professionals in immersive media and 3D audio. This event will foster meaningful international collaborations—across academia, industry, heritage and museum institutions, the arts, and environmental initiatives—while driving forward innovation in immersive media.
