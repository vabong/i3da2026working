I3DA: Immersive Media in Heritage, Nature and Consciousness

I3DA 2026 will focus on innovation in 3D immersive audio, including virtual acoustics, architectural and musical acoustics, and soundscape design. This special edition will include visual and interactive aspects of immersive media, such as LIDAR scans, photogrammetry, and digital archaeology.

The conference fosters international collaborations across academia, industry, heritage institutions, and environmental initiatives.
