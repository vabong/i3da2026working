Call for Papers

We cordially invite you to submit your papers and demos for the International Conference on Immersive and 3D Audio (I3DA 2026). The Organizing Committee is pleased to announce that I3DA 2026 will be hosted in Venice, at Palazzo Giustinian Lolin, the headquarters of the Fondazione Ugo e Olga Levi and the University of Warwick Venice Centre.

Theme: Immersive Media in Heritage, Nature and Consciousness. I3DA 2026 focuses on immersive media and spatial audio research across cultural heritage, natural environments, and human perception, including spatial audio algorithms and systems, auralization and digital twins, digital archaeology, soundscapes and bioacoustics, and perception and cognition in immersive contexts.

Special Edition Focus

Furthermore, I3DA 2026 will be a special edition, that will include visual and interactive aspects of immersive media, such as related computer graphics, LIDAR scans, photogrammetry, immersive world building, digital archaeology, 3D visualization, and hardware and software applications across both fields.

Topics of Interest

Topics include (but are not limited to):

  • Spatial audio algorithms & systems (binaural, Ambisonics, object-based; real-time and cross-platform)
  • Auralization, virtual acoustics & acoustic digital twins (including heritage and environmental spaces)
  • Architectural acoustics & immersive space design (theaters, churches, studios, heritage performance spaces)
  • Spatial capture & measurement (HOA arrays, soundscapes, bioacoustics, hydrophones, wearable sensing)
  • Visual interaction, XR & digital archaeology (LiDAR, photogrammetry, 3D scanning, audio-visual pipelines)
  • Signal processing, AI & nonlinear acoustics (rendering, personalization, separation, modeling, reproduction)
  • Perception, cognition & consciousness (presence, emotion, listening tests, EEG/biometrics)
  • Nature, bioacoustics & eco-acoustics (ecosystems, lagoon/marine studies, fragile environments)
  • Creative practice & immersive media (installations, XR storytelling, site-specific experiences)

How to Submit a Paper

To submit your full paper, please log into your SUBMISSION, click ADD FILES on the top-right hand side and add your file. The papers must be submitted via EasyChair paper management system, following the IEEE guidelines for proceedings papers. Authors must follow IEEE ethical and writing guidelines.

Anti-Plagiarism Screening

We will conduct to the submitted papers an anti-plagiarism screening to ensure that all papers are clear, authentic and relevant to the conference material. We submit all papers to the Turnitin and/or Compilatio Scanner. Please note that once a paper is submitted to I3DA, it cannot be presented to other journals/conferences.
