Partner with I3DA 2026

The International Conference on Immersive and 3D Audio (I3DA 2026) is a high-level international forum dedicated to advanced research and applied innovation in spatial and immersive audio. It convenes researchers, engineers, laboratory directors, acoustic consultants, VR/AR developers, and industry specialists actively shaping the future of multichannel and 3D sound environments.

Held in Venice from 19–22 November 2026, the conference provides a focused platform for technical exchange and direct engagement with decision-makers responsible for specifying, deploying, and evaluating immersive audio systems worldwide.
