Partner with I3DA 2026

The International Conference on Immersive and 3D Audio (I3DA 2026) is a high-level international forum dedicated to advanced research and applied innovation in spatial and immersive audio. The conference convenes leading researchers, engineers, laboratory directors, acoustic consultants, VR/AR developers, and industry specialists actively shaping the future of multichannel and 3D sound environments.

Held in Venice from 19–22 November 2026, I3DA provides a focused, technically rigorous platform for direct engagement with decision-makers responsible for specifying, deploying, and evaluating immersive audio systems in research laboratories, cultural institutions, performance spaces, and advanced media environments.

Partnering with I3DA 2026 offers structured visibility, technical activation opportunities, and positioning within an expert community at the forefront of immersive audio development.

All agreements are formalized through Consorzio Futuro in Ricerca (CFR).
