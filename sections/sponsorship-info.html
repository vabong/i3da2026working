Sponsorship & Partnership

I3DA 2026 is the premier global forum for researchers, engineers, and industry leaders in spatial audio, immersive media, and IoT-driven acoustic environments.

Partnering with us in Venice provides direct access to a highly specialized audience at the intersection of heritage, technology, and creative innovation.
